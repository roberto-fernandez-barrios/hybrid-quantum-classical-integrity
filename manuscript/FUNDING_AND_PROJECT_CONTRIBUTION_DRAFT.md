## Project-contribution statement for report/supplement

This artifact contributes to ATHENA-AEGIS as follows:

- **G3.1:** controlled quantum-workflow mutations, sensor blind regions,
  layered countermeasures and a fail-closed demonstrator;
- **G3.2:** validation contracts spanning data, preprocessing, simulated
  circuit/transpilation, kernel estimation, prediction, labels and reporting;
- **G3.3:** information-set conditional auditability, integrity metrics,
  coverage evidence and executable response rules;
- **Result 3.1 [SOFTWARE]:** installable research package, console entry point,
  runners, builders, tests, CI, hash manifests and HSaaS contract prototype;
- **Result 3.2 [REPORT]:** manuscript, validation notes, threat-model card,
  reproduction guide and requirement-to-evidence traceability matrix;
- **WP5 Task 5.2:** scientific dissemination through the submitted article and
  its archived research artifact.

The paper does **not** cover WP5 Task 5.1 or Result 5.1 (the operational Fleet
Management case study). The use of network-security datasets and the local
HSaaS contract demonstrator is not evidence of Fleet Management implementation.

The statement must retain the explicit boundary that scheduling/provider
security, calibrated backend noise, multi-tenancy and QPU execution are not yet
demonstrated by this release.
