# Version 1.3.5 — Targeted methodological sensitivity amendment

Version DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22678092. Concept DOI:
10.5281/zenodo.22550852.

Version 1.3.5 adds one preregistered geometry-aligned sensitivity analysis to
resolve the relationship between clean-resample calibration and same-item
intervention scoring. No dataset, model family, kernel, feature map,
hyperparameter search, threat class, formal theorem or primary structural
experiment was changed.

The audit confirmed that the original frozen design calibrated batch sensors
with clean resamples while feature interventions were scored against a
protected clean same-item set. The separate sensitivity holds the
reference/current relationship and batch construction fixed between clean and
intervened cases. It covers all eight environments, 240 deterministic frozen
jobs, 22 existing interventions and 13,200 observations.

Mean shift and scaling remain highly detectable; dropout remains
strength-dependent. Exact paired identity response is zero and is reported
separately from the clean-resample false-action rate. Across the prespecified
matched comparisons, the adaptive cluster-preserving response is lower than
its control in 25–40 of 40 environment/split cells and never higher while
retaining material changes. Gate A therefore survives qualitatively, with a
bounded interpretation: calibration controls clean-resample false actions but
does not guarantee coverage against the declared monitor-aware attacker.

The manuscript also clarifies that minimum evidence granularity is relative to
the declared reference lattice, that compact authenticated representations do
not erase item-aligned information, and that trusted aggregate generation is
temporally distinct from audit-time retention. The formal core is unchanged.
