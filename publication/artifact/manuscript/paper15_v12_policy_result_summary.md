## Amendment A1 — a real finding of the audit

`impact_bal_acc` in the 1.0.0/1.1.x tables is the positive part
$\max(\mathrm{BA}_0 - \mathrm{BA}_a, 0)$. The 1.1.1 sentence "no negative
impact occurs" therefore referred to a clipped column. Under the signed
definition the frozen expansion contains, among the 3,600 label-path rows,
**2,184 with a lowered, 983 with an unchanged and 433 with a raised balanced
accuracy** (Gate 1: 1,276 / 105 / 59). The material label-path count under
$|\Delta_R| > 0$ is 2,617, not 2,184; all 2,617 have non-zero item-aligned
confusion evidence (witness W8), and the 2,184 remain a subset. The
verifier now recomputes the signed counts. This does not weaken the claim
(an apparent improvement caused by corruption is a conclusion change and is
caught by the same evidence); it corrects an inaccurate sentence.

## Gate F — family-wise (decision-level) calibration

**Design realized.** 60 cells × 4 batch-level regimes; family threshold =
191st smallest calibration family score (max rank score over the regime);
thresholds from the 12,000 calibration draws only; false alarms on the 12,000
disjoint evaluation draws. Inference unit: (environment, split-seed) cluster,
40 clusters, 5 per environment; pooled rates are descriptive.

**F1 — decision false-alarm rate (evaluation draws, nominal 0.05 per decision).**

| Regime | Union rule (1.1.0) | Family rule (Gate F) | Family rule, environment cluster means (min–max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| I_X (3 sensors) | 0.125 | 0.061 | 0.025–0.133 |
| I_XF (6) | 0.203 | 0.073 | 0.045–0.125 |
| I_Ym (2, perfectly dependent) | 0.048 | 0.048 | 0.025–0.070 |
| I_XFY (10) | 0.259 | 0.079 | 0.046–0.146 |

Per-environment five-cluster $t$ intervals are in
`family_false_alarm_by_environment.csv` (Table S in the supplement). The
expectation recorded before analysis ("near 0.05 in every regime") was met
only approximately: the family rule stays 1.2–1.6× the nominal level in the
pooled rate, with the excess concentrated in E1 (256-row draws from 322-row
halves; I_XFY 0.146) and E3 ToN-IoT (25.7 % near-duplicate rows; 0.102). The
residual excess measures the violation of the exchangeability premise
(overlapping draws; calibration and evaluation halves are different rows of
one staged table). I_Ym is unchanged because label prior shift and label JSD
are monotone transforms of each other.

**F2 — detection cost of calibration (pooled over 600 observations per row).**
Mean shift and scaling drift stay at 0.98–1.00 in every batch regime; sign
flip falls from 0.12/0.53/0.76 to 0.09/0.47/0.72 (I_X) and from
0.19/0.64/0.88 to 0.15/0.56/0.85 (I_XF); dropout stays at 0.00–0.04; label
paths stay at 0 in I_X/I_XF/I_Ym and fall from 0.01–0.03 to 0.00–0.02 in
batch-level I_XFY. Near-null shams fire the family rule in 43–68 % of cells
(union: 65–86 %).

**F3 — label-path consistency (checks).** Family-calibrated I_X and I_XF fire
0/3,600; I_Ym fires 0/1,800 on prior-preserving rows. Batch-level I_XFY fires
on 43 (union) / 16 (family) of the 2,617 material label rows (0.016 / 0.006);
the item-aligned auditor detects 2,617/2,617.

**Where the unsafe allows come from (P2).** Label path: 2,617/2,617 served

## Interpretation and boundary

- Per-sensor calibration does not calibrate the decision; family calibration
  restores a decision-level budget at a small detection cost, and its residual
  excess over nominal is a measured property of the null design, not hidden.
- The unsafe-allow count is set by the information regime, not by the
  threshold: three fifths of the materially changed results are served by the
  batch-level regimes with feature evidence (the label-marginal regime serves
  all of them), and only the trusted-reference regime serves none. By
  Corollary 3 the aggregate confusion reference alone suffices for that
  (W9: 2,792 of 3,600 label rows detected, all 2,617 material ones); the
  item-aligned reference is needed for the 808 aggregate-preserving
  relabelings (W10).
- These are fixed-design, simulator-only results on frozen observations.
  Context-conditioned runtime calibration, abstention with recovery, real-QPU
  execution and service-level evaluation remain outside this article.
