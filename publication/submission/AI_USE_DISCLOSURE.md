# Generative-AI disclosure (1.1.0 wording approved by all authors on 2026-09-06; extended for 1.2.0 on 2026-09-07)

Text used in the manuscript Acknowledgment and in the submission portal:

> During manuscript and artifact preparation the authors used two generative-AI
> coding assistants. OpenAI Codex (May–August 2026) assisted with literature
> discovery, drafting and language editing of the Markdown manuscript and its
> TDSC conversion, code inspection, and the evidence builders, verifier and
> tests of artifact 1.0.0. Anthropic Claude Code (September 2026) assisted with
> the related-work additions, the formal section and its counterexamples, the
> adversary model, the abstract, results, limitations and conclusion edits, the
> supplement sections on the calibration and policy gates and their generated
> tables, and the artifact 1.1.0 and 1.2.0 code (null-control sampler,
> cross-validated tuning, execution queue, evidence builders, family-wise
> calibration, policy layer, figure and table generators, artifact assembler
> and their tests). The authors specified the protocols, verified the primary
> sources, numerical results, code and generated text, and retain full
> responsibility for the article.

This wording identifies each system, the affected sections and the level of
use, as the IEEE editorial policy requires for AI-generated content. The
preregistrations of both gate sets, their amendment records and the numerical
results were reviewed by the authors before inclusion. The 1.2.0 extension of
this statement (formal core, adversary model, policy gates and their code)
must be re-approved by all authors before upload.
